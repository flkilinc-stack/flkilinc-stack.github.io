Blocko — Delete Your Data

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Blocko has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. All of your game data is either stored on your own device or held by Google as the provider of ads, analytics and leaderboards. Here is how to remove each type.

Fastest option — delete everything

Uninstall the app. This permanently deletes all game data stored on your device: high score, level progress, Adventure stars, coins, power-ups, unlocked themes and settings. Nothing is kept on our side, so there is nothing left for us to hold.

1. Game progress on your device

Choose either:

  • Uninstall the app — removes everything, or
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Blocko → Storage → Clear storage. This resets the game to a fresh install while keeping the app.
  • iOS: iOS does not offer a per-app storage reset. Delete the app and reinstall it to reset progress.

2. Analytics data

Analytics are off unless you switched them on. To stop collection and have existing data deleted:

  1. Open Blocko → Settings → turn Usage Stats off. No further events are sent from that moment.
  2. To also erase events already collected, email us (see below) with the subject "Analytics deletion request". We will delete the associated records from Firebase within 30 days.

Note: analytics data is tied to a random app instance ID, not to you personally. Uninstalling the app resets that ID, and Google automatically expires the data after 14 months.

3. Advertising data

Ad data is held by Google, not by us. You control it directly:

  • Withdraw ad consent (EEA/UK/CH): Blocko → Settings → Privacy Options → change your choice.
  • iOS tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn Blocko off.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. iOS: turning tracking off blocks access to the IDFA.
  • Ask Google to delete ad data: see Google's Privacy Policy and My Ad Center.

4. Leaderboard data (Android)

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your score entry is stored by Google. To remove it: open the Play Games app → Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or delete only this game's data from the same screen. You can also simply not sign in — no score is sent unless you do.

5. Email request

Request deletion by email

Send a message to kilikk.gamess@gmail.com with the subject "Blocko data deletion request".

Please tell us which data you mean (analytics, leaderboard, or both). Since we hold no account, we may ask for the approximate date and device model so we can locate the right records.

Response time: within 30 days.
